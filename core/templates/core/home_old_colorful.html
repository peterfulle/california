Our Mission

Bridging the Gap in Chilean Startup Internationalization

Chile currently has limited capabilities to facilitate fast, validated, and efficient connections between startups and investors in highly competitive markets like Silicon Valley. This forces entrepreneurs to face their landing in new markets with their own resources and without a structure to accompany and scale their insertion.

Chile Conecta fills this gap by offering a system adapted to Chilean startups, with traceability, metrics, and institutional backing from CORFO and ProChile, transforming isolated efforts into a strategic tool that facilitates more successful landings in global hubs like California.
